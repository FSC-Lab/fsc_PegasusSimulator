2. Q1 · Trigger the drop on end-effector error

Yes, but gate on the basket and the wrist as well as the claw, and wait out the arrival swing. The GS opens the gripper on the Drop click with no check at all.
  • D-1's click came 0.67 s after the drop leg ended, with the claw 46–48 mm from its target. The planner's tolerance is 50 mm, so it read "within tolerance".
  • The claw is still swinging at that point. In E-1 the claw error rang ±47 mm half a second after the leg ended and reached 8 mm only at +1.8 s.
  • The basket left the jaws 38 mm from the drop point (−26, +28 mm), moving 0.06 m/s sideways and rolled +4.5° by the wrist swing. It fell 0.2 m, bounced 67 mm in +y on impact (tilting to 22°) and came to rest 103 mm off, tilted 9° with a marker ball on the rim.
  • Over the last 1.5 s before the click, the ballistic landing point never came closer than 30 mm. No instant in that window would have hit the centre.

Proposed trigger, built like the planner's existing automatic descent for the pick (pick_place_descent_on_request: false):

  1. At least 2 s after the drop leg ends.
  2. Claw error ≤ 20 mm for 0.5 s, claw nearly still.
  3. The basket's own mocap position (obj_0) within about 25 mm of the drop point and slow. The basket swings ±40 mm across the claw, so a claw-only gate cannot see where it is.
  4. q4 near 0°, so the basket is level when released.
  5. On timeout, hold and report. Never open on a timer. The GS Drop button stays as the manual override.

3. Q2 · Arm motion in the two transits

The arm's up/down motion is not what pitches the drone. E-1 flew the identical arm motion with almost no oscillation. With the basket, the wrist roll q4 swings and drives the vehicle.

Oscillation = PX4 attitude band-passed 0.3–2 Hz. The lift row is the payload's weight arriving with no feed-forward (section 4).

  • The wrist was stuck, then broke loose. q4 sat at exactly 0° (static friction) for 16 s after the pick, while the arm folded and the vehicle turned, and pitch stayed near 0.5°. It broke loose at 57.3 s, as the 180° turn was slowing down.
  • j3 was at its cap just before. For the 3.7 s before the break, j3 sat at its 1.42 N·m cap during the loaded fold to the carry pose (it needed 1.74 N·m) and lagged 5°.
  • From then until the drop, everything moved at ~0.5 Hz: q4 ±10–20°, the basket 80 mm p-p across the claw, and the vehicle's roll and pitch ±3–4°.
  • The swing is left over, not forced. The planned sideways acceleration of the claw on the drop leg is only 0.01 m/s² rms, so the swing on that leg is carried over from the turn, not driven by the leg.

4. Q3 · Arm compensation terms

Yes. The wrist roll (j4) is effectively undamped, and the payload load steps have no feed-forward.

  • The back-EMF term dominates j4's command. The feed-forward is 83–91 duty counts rms on j4 against 4.5 from the law. The measured-velocity friction relay cancels the gearbox friction as well. Net damping on q4 is about zero, slightly negative.
  • The same cause parks q4 after the release. With ~0.05 N·m/rad of stiffness against ~0.05 N·m of static friction, q4 can sit about 1 rad off before the law's torque moves it.

Load steps and the j3 cap

  • Lift: centre-of-mass error peaked at 241 mm (14.5 mm in E-1) and q2 sagged 9.7°. The observer's joint estimate builds to −0.74 N·m on j2/j3 over the following seconds.
  • Release: centre-of-mass error peaked at 195 mm.
  • j3 cap: the j3 command was above its 1.42 N·m cap for 3.7 s during the loaded fold-up, peaking at 1.74 N·m.
  • No change needed for j2/j3 friction and gravity correction: 0.15–0.20 N·m rms, no clipping outside the fold-up, unloaded tracking unchanged.

D-2's pick failure

The wrist was not involved: q4 stayed at 0°. The jaws held the fin 16 mm further out than in D-1 (basket 89 vs 73 mm along the claw), about twice the lever. The basket pivoted out during the climb. Both grasps closed inside the GS's 20 mm close gate.

5. What to change, in order

  1. Damp the wrist. Keep part of j4's own damping: back-EMF compensation scale about 0.7–0.8 on j4 only, 1.0 on j1–j3. This needs a per-joint version of back_emf_ff_scale; lowering back_emf_ke[3] works from config but mislabels a measured constant. Bench check: hold the basket, flick the wrist, watch the swing decay.
  2. Set the carry pose equal to the pick/drop pose [0, 32, 38, 0]. This removes the 12° q1 sideways swing and the loaded fold that put j3 on its cap. Optionally slow or shorten the loaded 180° turn (peak 17.7°/s).
  3. Automatic drop trigger in the planner with the gates in section 2, Drop button as override.
  4. Payload feed-forward switched by the grasp and open results, to cut the 241 mm lift and 195 mm release lurches (a law change).
  5. Grip: a tighter close gate along the claw axis and a deeper or keyed grip on the fin.

Basis: one loaded drop and one unloaded control, so treat the numbers as indicative. How long a 20 mm gate would wait with the basket is not measured; the loaded hold before the click was 0.67 s. Nothing in the code or configuration has been changed. Analysis, scripts and numbers: docs/docs_aerial_manipulator/archive/wb_pick_drop_flight_20261015/.
