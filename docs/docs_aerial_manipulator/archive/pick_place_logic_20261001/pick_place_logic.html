1The mission in one paragraph

The mission is six rest-to-rest whole-body moves, each started by its own button in the arm ground station's Pick & Place tab. When the operator presses Plan, the planner computes all six goal poses once. They come from nominal base poses in its parameters (shifted by an Adjust offset) and two points captured from motion capture: the object obj_0 and the drop point drop_0. After that, every leg button re-plans that one leg from wherever the vehicle is holding and flies it straight away, with no separate Send. After the two claw legs, the separate Pick & Place PS4 tab lets the operator nudge the claw with the sticks (the arm moves, the drone holds) and work the gripper with R1 / L1. The planner never touches the gripper, PX4 modes, arming or the touchdown.

  • Legs6, flown in order
  • Arrival gateclaw within 50 mm
  • Capture0.5 s mean, ≤ 20 mm spread
  • Claw-down grasp point0.155 m ahead, 0.340 m below
  • Place-leg arm sweep19.2 s on the test scene
  • Final hover0.8 m over the land spot

2What the operator does, step by step

Letters are setup steps; numbers are the six leg buttons. The chips name which station or node each step goes through.

  1. A
    Take off in SAFETYdrone GS

    The usual baseline takeoff. Nothing in pick-and-place runs yet; the tab shows NOT IN DIRECT.

  2. B
    Adjust, with the vehicle on the physical start markPick & Place tabplanner

    Offset = measured position − nominal start (x and y only, unless pick_place_adjust_z / _yaw are set). Every nominal base pose is shifted by it, so the day's mocap centring does not matter. Works on the ground or in a hover, in SAFETY or DIRECT.

  3. C
    Get obj_0 and Get drop_0Pick & Place tabplanner

    Each averages its mocap body over the last 0.5 s and is refused if the samples scatter more than 20 mm. Also works in SAFETY. The four "Get (opt.)" base-point buttons are refused today because no mocap topic is set for them.

  4. D
    Enter whole-body DIRECTdrone GSplanner

    The planner captures its HOLD from odometry and the arm encoders and starts streaming. The tab shows NOT PLANNED: press Plan, or names the missing capture.

  5. E
    PlanPick & Place tabplanner

    The six goals are fixed and the whole mission is dry-run from the current hold. Shows PLANNING, then READY next=go_to_start. The tab draws the planned claw and airframe paths in its 3-D view and lists each leg's duration on its button.

  6. 1–6
    Press the legs in orderPick & Place tabplannerflight node

    The next leg's button is outlined green. Each press re-plans that leg from where the vehicle holds now, flies it (FLYING go_to_start T=7.4s), and ends in a HOLD at the leg's goal (DONE go_to_start next=execute_pick). A finished leg may be flown again; a leg cannot be skipped.

  7. F
    After leg 2 and leg 4: optional fine correction and the gripperPick & Place PS4 tabplannergripper action

    When the status ends in "claw within tolerance: fine correction OK", the operator may engage the PS4 tab. The sticks nudge the claw (the arm moves, the drone holds); R1 closes and L1 opens the gripper. Pressing the next leg disengages the tab automatically.

  8. G
    After leg 6: land by handoperatordrone GS

    The status reads COMPLETE -- touch down with SAFETY -> land. The vehicle is hovering 0.8 m over the landing spot; the operator reverts to SAFETY and lands.

4Inputs: poses, Adjust and captures

4.1Parameters and their defaults

Base poses are [x, y, z, yaw°] in the world frame with the actual yaw (the drone GS's convention). Arm poses are joint angles in degrees.

The flight configs do not set any of these. No fsc_autopilot_ros2 whole-body yaml (hardware, mirror sim, robustness sim, PS4 test) carries a pick_place_* key, and the stacks start the planner with those yamls. A real or simulated run therefore uses the code defaults above, and the three base poses are placeholders for the room.

4.2Adjust

  • Needs fresh odometry. Is refused while a leg is in flight.
  • Offset = measured body position − nominal start. z is shifted only if pick_place_adjust_z is true; yaw only if pick_place_adjust_yaw is true (measured from PX4 attitude).
  • The offset is added to all four nominal base poses. It is not added to the two captured claw points, which are measured in the world already.
  • Pressing Adjust throws away any existing plan.

4.3Captures (the Get buttons)

  • The planner keeps the last 2 s of each mocap body. A capture averages the samples from the last 0.5 s.
  • Refused if there are no samples in the window, if they spread more than 20 mm from their mean, or while a leg is in flight. The spread check exists because Isaac's emulator once published a second, phantom obj_0 at the origin and the average of the two was flown.
  • Pick and place are required before Plan. They are used as measured, with no Adjust offset.
  • A base point can be captured only if its _topic parameter is set (none is). If it were, its x and y would replace the nominal pose plus offset; z and yaw would stay nominal.
  • Every capture throws away any existing plan.
  • Mocap and odometry must share one world frame. On the EKF2-fused stack, EKF2's local origin must sit on the mocap origin.

5The Plan button

Plan is refused unless all of these hold: whole-body DIRECT with a hold; no leg in flight and the planner not executing; PS4 teleoperation not engaged; both claw points captured.

  1. The six goal poses are fixed. Each arm pose is checked against the joint box and against the singularity margin (σnd ≥ 0.10); a failure names the leg. Claw goals are worked out as in 6.1.
  2. The whole mission is dry-run on a worker thread: leg 1 from the current hold, each later leg from the goal of the leg before. The first infeasible leg stops it with INFEASIBLE: <leg>: <reason>.
  3. The result is published: the status becomes READY next=go_to_start, the info topic carries the goals and the six durations, and the path topics carry 600 samples each of the claw and the airframe.

The dry-run trajectories are only a preview. Flying a leg never reuses them: each leg is planned again from the current hold to the goal fixed here. What Plan really commits to is the set of six goals.

6.1Where a claw leg puts the vehicle

For legs 2 and 4 the planner chooses the body pose that puts the claw (the model's grasp point) exactly on the target, with the arm in its fixed pose:

target = captured point + offset (world) r0e(q) = grasp point in the body frame for the arm pose q bearing = direction from the previous leg's GOAL body position to the target alpha = azimuth of r0e(q) in the body frame (nose, if the arm has no horizontal reach) heading = bearing − alpha so the arm's reach points at the target body = target − Rz(heading) · r0e(q)

The vehicle therefore turns to face the object over the whole leg; the flat planner keeps the yaw smooth and within its rate bound w_max. The bearing is taken from the previous leg's planned goal, not from where the vehicle actually is, and the goal is fixed at Plan.

6.2The claw-down pose [0, 0, 0, 0]

Upper arm hanging straight down, forearm out along the nose, claw pointing straight down. The grasp point is 0.155 m ahead of and 0.340 m below the body origin, with σnd = 0.169, above the 0.10 margin. This is the bottom of the arm's reach: from it, the arm alone can raise the claw, pull it in or swing it sideways, but cannot lower it or push it further out. Lowering the claw onto an object is the body's job, and the leg's plan does it.

6.3The retreat before legs 3 and 5

The leg first flies a separate rest-to-rest move: from the current hold, climb 0.15 m and back off 0.20 m along the reverse of the nose, with the arm unchanged. Then a second transition goes to the leg's goal. The two are streamed back to back as one leg. Setting both distances to zero removes the retreat.

6.4The arm sweep in leg 4

Leg 4 is not a normal transition. Every channel is written down in closed form and turned into a compatible reference directly, with no fixed-point solve:

  • The system centre of mass goes in a straight line from start to goal on a 9th-order (nonic) phase, at rest through snap at both ends, so the body-torque reference has no step at the joins.
  • The heading turns on the same phase.
  • q3 and q4 go straight from the carry pose to the place pose.
  • q1 and q2 follow that straight line blended with a sinusoid inside a plateau window: q1 between −25° and +25°, q2 between 10° and 45°, a quarter cycle apart, 2 full cycles. The window ramps in and out over 20 % of the duration each.
  • The duration is the shortest one (8 to 120 s, bisected to 2 %) that passes every check on a 401-point grid: joint box, σnd, CoM speed and acceleration, yaw rate, joint rate ≤ 0.5 rad/s, joint torque and rotor force. On the test scene it is 19.2 s, limited by the joint rate, and takes 48 ms to plan.

The point of the leg is to show the whole-body law carrying a payload that moves in 3-D while the vehicle travels: the airframe moves to absorb the arm's centre-of-mass excursion.

6.5What one leg button does

  1. Checks, in this order: DIRECT with a hold; the mission not still planning; a plan exists; PS4 teleoperation not engaged; the planner not CALCULATING or EXECUTING; leg ≤ last completed + 1 (else "out of order -- the next leg is …").
  2. Plans the leg from the current hold to the goal fixed at Plan. The planner state becomes CALCULATING and the status FLYING <leg>.
  3. Executes at once, with no Send step. The planner state becomes EXECUTING and the status gains the duration.
  4. Holds at the goal when the trajectory ends. The leg counts as completed; the status becomes DONE <leg> next=<leg>.
  5. If the leg is infeasible, the status becomes INFEASIBLE: <leg>: <reason>, progress is unchanged and the vehicle keeps holding. The button can be pressed again.

7Arrival gate and the PS4 fine correction

7.1The arrival gate

At 10 Hz the planner publishes pick_place/arrival_error for the leg in flight, or else the last leg completed:

  • Claw legs (2, 4): the measured claw, from forward kinematics on the measured joints at the measured body pose (odometry + PX4 attitude), against the goal claw point.
  • Other legs: the measured body origin against the goal body position.
  • Within = error ≤ 50 mm. Settled = within, no leg in flight, and the planner in HOLD.

After a claw leg the status appends "claw within tolerance: fine correction OK" when settled, otherwise "claw outside tolerance". The Pick & Place tab shows "Claw error N mm (tolerance 50 mm) after Execute To Pick", in green when within.

7.2When the PS4 tab will engage

Engage is refused, with the reason shown, unless every check passes, in this order:

  1. The planner is in whole-body DIRECT.
  2. The planner is not moving (not CALCULATING, PLANNED or EXECUTING).
  3. An arrival message arrived in the last 0.6 s.
  4. The last leg was Execute To Pick or Execute To Place.
  5. The gate is settled: claw within 50 mm and the planner holding.
  6. The measured claw (current_ee) and its heading (current_ee_heading) are fresh (≤ 0.5 s).
  7. The workspace grid and the base anchor have arrived from the planner.

Engaging seeds the target on the measured claw position and the measured claw heading, so engaging moves nothing and a position nudge never swings the wrist.

Stick deadzone is 0.08. Each axis of the target is limited on its own, so a blocked axis does not stop the others. A step is kept only if it stays within ±0.10 m (adjustable 0.02–0.20) of the engage point on that axis, and inside pick_place/workspace_rz. That grid is the reachable (r, z) region about the planner's base anchor, with its floor lowered to −0.345 m so the claw-down pose is inside it.

7.4One stick push is one short arm move

  1. The tab integrates the sticks into a target. When it has moved 5 mm or 2° from the last target the planner accepted, the tab publishes it as an EE target.
  2. The planner solves it with the body held where it is (an arm-only change) and reports PLANNED.
  3. The tab calls Send itself; the planner executes and returns to HOLD. The sticks are frozen while the planner is busy.
  4. If the planner refuses the target, the tab snaps back to the last accepted one and shows the reason. If PLANNED does not come within 5 s, it gives up and asks for another push.

7.5When the tab lets go

  • The vehicle leaves whole-body DIRECT.
  • A pick-and-place leg starts (the status turns to FLYING).
  • The planner starts any move the tab did not send.
  • The operator engages the PS4 Remote tab. One pad drives one tab: engaging either one drops the other (but see P6).

8.2When the Pick & Place tab's buttons are enabled

  • Adjust, Get, Reset: whenever no leg is in flight and the mission is not planning.
  • Plan: as above, and the planner idle (HOLD, PLANNED, PENDING or INFEASIBLE).

8.4The planner's other modes

  • A drone-GS target, an EE target or Go Home sent while a leg is still being planned cancels that leg. While a leg executes, they are ignored.
  • PS4 teleoperation (the PS4 Remote tab): Plan and the legs are refused while it is engaged, and engaging it is refused while a leg is planning or executing. These two refusals were added in the merge of main; the feature branch had no teleop.

9What the mode leaves to the operator

  • The gripper. No leg opens or closes it.
  • Takeoff, landing, arming and PX4 modes. The mission starts from a DIRECT hover and ends in a 0.8 m hover.
  • Stopping mid-leg. There is no pause or abort button in the mode; the only way out is a revert to SAFETY. Reset is refused while a leg flies.
  • Contact. Nothing in the mode knows whether the claw touched or holds the object; the flight node runs its free-flight observer settings throughout.
  • Obstacles. The plan checks the vehicle's own limits (joints, singularity, speeds, torques, rotor forces), not the room. The retreat is the only table-clearing logic.

11Points to decide on

These are what I noticed while reading the code against how it would be flown. They are observations for your review, not changes I have made.

  1. The base poses are placeholders, and no flight config sets them.

    Start (0, 0, 1.0), place start (0, −1.0, 1.0), land start (−1.0, 0, 1.0) and land (−1.0, 0, 0.8) come from the code defaults on every stack.

  2. The gripper is not part of the sequence.

    Closing at the pick and opening at the place depend on the operator remembering R1 / L1 or the GS buttons. Nothing blocks leg 3 with the gripper open.

  3. The claw goes exactly to the marker point.

    With both offsets at zero, the grasp point lands on the mocap body's origin. Where that marker sits relative to the part to be grasped decides the grasp.

  4. The pick pose is the bottom of the arm's reach.

    With the claw down at [0, 0, 0, 0], the fine correction can raise the claw, pull it in or move it sideways, but cannot lower it or push it further out. A grasp that needs the claw a few millimetres lower needs the body to move.

  5. The fine-correction sticks are in world axes, and the left/right sign disagrees with the comment.

    The PS4 Remote teleop moves the claw relative to the airframe; this tab moves it along world X / Y. Pushing the left stick left reads +1 and the code applies −Y, while the tab's own comment says left is +Y. No test pushes the left stick.

  6. The handover from PS4 Remote back to the fine tab is broken on the merged planner.

    Engaging PS4 Remote now really puts the planner into teleop. The fine tab can only engage while the planner holds, so it refuses, its "engaging drops the other" never fires, and it shows the misleading "claw 0 mm from the target … wait for it to settle". The operator must release PS4 Remote and wait for HOLD first.

  7. The gate needs a still hold.

    On the stressed sim plant the DIRECT hold oscillated by 115–226 mm and the gate flickered, so the fine correction could not engage at all.

  8. Claw-down holds and the sweep excite an instability seen in Isaac.

    The watchdog tripped on both plants with the arm extended. It is a flight-law question; on the planning side only a slower or narrower sweep, or a better-conditioned pick pose, would excite it less.

  9. There is no pause or abort inside the mode.

    A leg in flight can only be stopped by reverting to SAFETY.

  10. The landing is manual and the 0.8 m final hover is unflown.

    After COMPLETE the operator lands from SAFETY.

  11. Base-point captures are wired but inactive.

    The four "Get (opt.)" buttons always refuse because no topic is configured for them.

  12. Claw-leg headings are fixed at Plan.

    The bearing for legs 2 and 4 is taken from the previous leg's planned goal. If a later leg starts somewhere else (after a fine correction or a re-flown leg), the vehicle still ends on the heading chosen at Plan.
