1.2

Simulation against flight

Each flight was replayed in Isaac with its own reference, the as-flown controller (kω 1.5, Ky 20) and the identified plant. The sim's time is plant time. Each planner leg is warped onto the flight's leg by the plan's own path progress. The sim's circle, anchored at a different place and heading, is rotated 132.7° and shifted onto the flight's; after that the two plans agree to 0.4 mm.
