3.1 Choosing the figure-8

The brief was a mean end-effector speed of about 0.20 m/s (the circle's is 0.131 m/s); a figure-8 "radius" of 0.60–0.75 m to fit the flight area, taken as the half-length A (the planner's counterpart of the circle radius, 0.5 m in earlier figure-8 runs); and the arm's q2 swinging on a period that divides the lap, four periods per lap as on the circle. The half-width is B = A/2, the planner's default proportion, which makes each lobe as wide as it is long. At a fixed mean speed a smaller 8 turns harder. The planner's own numbers for each candidate, at 0.20 m/s and time scale 1:

{SIZING}

Where the path turns fastest (the top and bottom of each lobe) the airframe swings around the end effector, so the CoM speed peaks above the end effector's 0.29 m/s. The planner's CoM speed bound of 0.30 m/s therefore rules out A = 0.60 and 0.65 m at this speed (smax below 1). A = 0.75 m is the largest size in the range and has the most margin (smax 1.07), with the lowest yaw rate and CoM acceleration. A thinner 8 (B = 0.30 or 0.225 m) would lower the peak yaw rate by only 6–12 %. Chosen: A = 0.75 m, B = 0.375 m, Tlap = 22.865 s (0.200 m/s mean), q2 = 25 ± 15° on a 5.716 s period, fold 55°, the circle's arm amplitude, centre and fold.

The planner's shared bounds (CoM acceleration 0.15 m/s², yaw rate 0.30 rad/s) would cap this shape at s = 0.34, i.e. 0.068 m/s. Those bounds also size every transition (go-to-start, back-to-origin, ground-station targets). Rather than raise them, the planner gained bounds for the end-effector run only: ee_traj_a_max = 0.40 m/s² and ee_traj_w_max = 1.0 rad/s (0 = inherit, so every other mode is unchanged). With them smax = 1.06, set by the shared 0.30 m/s CoM speed, and every flight below ran at s = 1.00.

Whole-body L1. The end-effector error rises from 15.0 mm on the circle to {wb_ee} mm, and the rise sits where the yaw rate is high: {wb_hi} mm RMS where |ψ̇| exceeds 35°/s against {wb_lo} mm where it is below 20°/s. The heading error stays at {wb_head}° RMS (peak {wb_headmax}°), and its dependence on the yaw rate is weak ({wb_slope} s), because the law receives the full reference chain, yaw rate and acceleration included, and feeds it forward. Most of its end-effector error is airframe position ({wb_af} mm of the split); the attitude and joint terms grow only from {wbc_att} / {wbc_j} mm on the circle to {wb_att} / {wb_j} mm.

Geometric L1 adaptive. The heading error grows from 2.97° to {geo_head}° RMS (peak {geo_headmax}°), and it is almost entirely the constant-yaw specialization's lag. A least-squares fit of the end-effector heading error on the reference yaw rate gives −{geo_slope} s (R² {geo_r2}), against the predicted Kω,z/KR,z = 0.411/1.737 = 0.237 s for ωd = 0; on the circle the same fit gives −{geoc_slope} s. At the figure-8's 51°/s that lag is 12–15°, and the position-mode arm carries it to the gripper. Through the arm's 0.23–0.28 m horizontal reach the same lag also moves the end-effector position: the airframe-attitude term of the split doubles from {geoc_att} mm on the circle to {geo_att} mm, about reach × heading error (0.25 m × {geo_head}° ≈ {geo_att_pred} mm). The end-effector position error rises from 48.9 to {geo_ee} mm, concentrated at the high yaw rates ({geo_hi} against {geo_lo} mm).

The rest of the geometric law's error is the offset section 2 already showed. Its L1 augmentation estimates the body-fixed lateral force (+0.55, −0.50) N but cannot cancel it, so the pure-P position loop parks the airframe F/Kp = (+27, −25) mm off in the body frame ({geo_off} mm before Start). Because the nose follows the path tangent, the body frame is the path frame, and the end effector rides {geo_along_r} mm ahead of and {geo_crossm_abs} mm to the right of its reference on average. On the circle the same offset showed as a wider, leading circle; on the figure-8 it shifts each lobe differently, since the right lobe turns clockwise and the left counter-clockwise.

On the circle the whole-body law tracked the end effector 3.3× better than the geometric law in position and 5.9× in heading; on the figure-8 the margins are {ratio_ee}× and {ratio_head}×.
