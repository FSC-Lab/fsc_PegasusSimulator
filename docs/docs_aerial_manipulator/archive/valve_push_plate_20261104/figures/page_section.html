5. A push plate for the closed claw

On 11-03 the open claw straddled the fin. The slide-in carried it about 18 mm past its grasp point, so the claw's bracket turned the valve before the turn began. The fin then turned inside the U and its back corner pressed the U's right rail sideways. That sideways push, 0.21 m ahead of the centre of mass, twisted the drone against all the yaw its rotors have, and the vehicle tipped.

This section adds one part and changes how the claw holds it. The claw is closed for the whole flight and pushes a flat plate on the fin's pushing edge with its nose, so the fin is never inside the claw. A nose on a flat face can only push. Sideways it slides, held back only by friction: about 0.1 N at the valve's 0.45 N of push, 0.02 N·m of yaw, where the jammed fin made 0.6 N·m or more.

    Making it

    Print and fit

    1. Print the plate and clip as one part (Valve_Assembly_v21.stl, the part on the fin's back edge), face down, 5 mm thick. Make the slot for the fin 5.3 mm wide.
    2. Slide it onto the fin's pushing edge (the side the drone comes from), its top flush with the fin's top. Drill 3.2 mm through the cheeks and the fin at about 165 mm above the table and fit one M3 bolt.
    3. Cover the face with PTFE tape. It keeps the sideways friction low, which is what keeps the yaw small.
    4. Leave the markers as they are: obj_0 and the axis offset do not change. The rotor gains 54.5 cm³, about 56 g printed like the rotor, and about 60 % more inertia about the shaft, since the plate sits out at the fin. That does not matter: the hub pad's friction does not change, and at the turn's speeds the push is friction, not inertia (under 1 mN·m of 72).

    What the software needs

    • The claw stays closed: the arm GS starts with -p push_pull_valve_cup_position:=-0.010 (the closed position), so Ready and the grasp gate move nothing.
    • The grasp point is the claw point that puts the nose 20 mm behind the face, 165 mm up: push_pull_ee_offset [0.160, -0.0613, 0.1704].
    • The tangent check at the nose: push_pull_turn_tangent_lead 0.0369 (new, 2026-11-04, default 0). The claw point sits 61 mm behind the fin's radial line, which the planner otherwise refuses as 21° off the tangent.
    • The fin guard describes the plate: push_pull_valve_fin = the plate's box, the push point 16.9 mm ahead of the claw point (push_pull_guard_fin_bracket_back -0.0169), across limit 35 mm.
    • The planned turn 97° for a 90° valve turn: the 20 mm slack costs 4–7°, and in Isaac the valve ended at 92.8–99.9°.
