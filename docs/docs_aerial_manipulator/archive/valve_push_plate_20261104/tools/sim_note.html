Reading the table.

  • Peak tilt runs from DIRECT entry and lowest q3 from Ready, both to the end of the run. Highest q3 covers the turn only, the window of the 40° jam limit.
  • Yaw command is the law's yaw torque in the turn. Sideways force is the controller's own estimate of the force on the claw across the vehicle, low-passed 0.5 s.
  • Nose on the plate is where the claw touched the plate in the turn: out from the shaft and up from the table. The plate spans 100–220 and 125–204 mm.
  • Before the turn gives the pushing point's closest approach to the face it pushes, from Ready's end, and how far the valve moved. The slack is 20 mm for the plate and 13 mm for the U push.
  • The abort reasons are the planner's guards, renamed here for the plate. The planner's own messages still say "fin" and "bracket".
