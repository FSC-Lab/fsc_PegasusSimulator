Twenty-two headless Isaac flights of the whole mission today, beside four U-push flights from 11-03. They used the v21 valve with the hub pad's measured 72 mN·m. Unless a row says otherwise, each flight used the shipped approach: the claw follows the vehicle down, settles, and is then held in the room for the turn. The arm GS flew every step. Every unforced room-held plate flight completed: nine of nine at two poses, nominal, drift seeds 2, 3 and 4, and an aim 12 mm off the fin's radius. Aimed 20 mm high, the depth guard stopped it before the turn. With no slack, the nose pushed the valve 15° in the hold and the station aborted when the hand-over did not settle. Every abort kept DIRECT with the tilt at or below 3.8°. So keep the 20 mm slack and aim on the fin's radius.
