# Simulation results for the paper

Isaac Sim runs of the T650 aerial manipulator, organised for plotting in MATLAB.

## Free-flight trajectory tracking

Three methods are compared:

- `whole_body_l1`: the proposed whole-body L1 impedance controller.
- `geometric_l1`: geometric control with L1 adaptive augmentation, "Geo-L1" (Cai et al., CEP 2025).
- `modular_adaptive`: modular adaptive control, "MAC" (Yadav et al., TMECH 2025).

Each method flies two end-effector trajectories at three mean EE speeds (0.10, 0.13 and 0.20 m/s), three runs each:

- **Circle**: radius 0.5 m.
- **Figure-8**: half-length 0.70 m, half-width 0.35 m, long axis on world x.

Every flight uses the same launch condition and the same plant:

- **Launch**: headless Isaac at real time (RTF 1), raw mocap feedback.
- **Plant**: the sim-to-real mirror plant (config A).
- **Planner**: one lap at time scale 1, EE-run bounds 0.40 m/s² and 1.0 rad/s.
- **Arm sweep**: q2 = 25 ± 15°, four cycles per lap, fold 55°.

Only the DIRECT control law differs between methods. For Geo-L1 the arm also runs in position mode, tracking the planner's joint reference.

```bash
# fly (resumes: completed runs on disk are skipped)
setsid nohup /usr/bin/python3 results/utils/run_tracking_campaign.py \
    > results/simulation_results/free_flight_tracking/campaign.out 2>&1 < /dev/null &
# score every completed run -> matlab_simulation_data/ + results/utils/tables/free_flight_tracking_sim.csv|json
/usr/bin/python3 results/utils/build_free_flight.py
# the paper table (results/utils/tables/free_flight_tracking_sim.tex; --paper <main.tex> also updates the paper)
/usr/bin/python3 results/utils/make_latex_table.py
```

The tools that fly, score and tabulate these runs, and their table outputs (`tables/`), are in
`results/utils/`.

`configs/` holds:

- **whole_body_l1**: a snapshot of the 4-D mirror sim yaml.
- **geometric_l1**: generated from it by `make_geometric_mirror_yaml.py` and `make_tuned_yaml.py`, with the 2026-10-01 tuned gains.
- **modular_adaptive**: generated by `make_modular_yaml.py`, with the `modular_final_gains_rt1.json` gains.

All three share every `sim_*` plant key, the planner section and the guards (tilt 15°).

### The `.mat` struct `run`

| field | content |
|---|---|
| `meta` | method, shape, nominal mean speed, lap time, trajectory size, arm sweep, plant, controller yaml, driver arguments, flight time, minimum RTF |
| `rmse` | this run's table numbers: platform position x/y/z and norm [mm], platform roll / pitch / heading [deg], EE position x/y/z and norm [mm], EE heading [deg], joints 1-4 [deg], plus CoM position, EE worst sample and peak tilt |
| `tracking` | 100 Hz signals over the trajectory run, `t` in s since the run started: measured, reference and error for the platform position and attitude, CoM, EE position and heading, and joints |
| `raw` | every recorded stream on the driver clock [s]: `odom`, `joints`, `reference` (the planner's whole-body reference), `planner_ee`, `arm_reference`, `controller_debug`, `events` |

Positions are in world ENU [m]. `platform_att_err_deg` is `[roll pitch heading]` in body axes.

### Metric definitions

These are the same definitions as the hardware report (`docs/docs_aerial_manipulator/archive/wb_vs_decoupled_flight_20260928/tools/metrics.py`).

- **Window**: the planner's trajectory run, ramps included, trimmed 0.1 s at each end.
- **Reference**: the planner's whole-body reference, which is identical for all three methods.
  - Platform position: `x_b = x_cd - R0 r_0c(q_d)`.
  - Platform attitude: the compatible attitude `R0` (thrust along `x_cd_ddot + g e3`, heading `b1_d`).
  - EE: position `r_ed`, heading `b1_de`. Joints: `q_d`.
- **Measured**: odometry plus joint states. The EE comes from the planner's own forward kinematics.
- **Errors**:
  - Platform attitude error: the rotation vector of `R_ref^T R`.
  - EE heading error: the azimuth difference.
  - Table cells: the mean over runs of each run's RMSE.

## Payload pick-and-place (2026-10-08/09)

The same scene and task for every controller (`application/robotic_arm/07_px4_t650_aerial_manipulator_pick_and_place.py`):
two 1 m pillars at (1, 1) and (−1, −1) m carrying the printed hat platform (top 1.008 m), the 200 g CAD basket
payload hooked by its wire hanger, the six-leg plan of the pick-and-place planner (go to start, pick, go to
place start, place, go to land start, land; the hook approach, the clockwise turns, the carry pose). Headless
Isaac at RTF 1, raw mocap, the mirror plant; each controller's `*_pick_and_place.yaml` (the free-flight
file + the pick-and-place gains: whole-body k_R/k_w 1.6/1.2, geometric kp/kv 15/10 and kp_z/kv_z 40/18).

```bash
# fly (resumes; one archive/pick_place_top_hat_20261008/tools/run_pnp.sh flight per attempt; closes the sim at the end)
setsid nohup results/utils/run_pick_and_place_campaign.sh 2 wb geo mod \
    > results/simulation_results/pick_and_place/campaign.out 2>&1 < /dev/null &
# score -> matlab_simulation_data/pick_and_place/ + results/utils/tables/pick_and_place_sim.csv|json|tex + figures
PYTHONNOUSERSITE=1 /usr/bin/python3 results/utils/build_pick_and_place.py [--paper <main.tex>]
```

### Metrics

- **Window**: each of the six legs from its start mark for the SHORTEST duration that leg was flown over
  all runs (identical for every controller, 78.1 s in total); the operator waits between legs are excluded.
- **UAV deviation** (Suarez et al. 2020): ‖ε_UAV‖ = ‖r_UAV^ref − r_UAV‖, max and RMS over the window, and
  ρ_UAV = ‖ε_UAV‖ / L with L = 0.37 m (the arm's reach). r_UAV^ref = x_cd − R0 r_0c(q_d) from the planner's
  reference stream, as in the free-flight metrics.
- **State RMSE**: the free-flight definitions (platform position xyz, attitude as the rotation vector of
  R_ref^T R, EE position xyz, EE heading, joints).

### Campaign outcome

|---|---|---|---|
| whole_body_l1 | 2 | 2 | basket placed 22.6 / 14.5 mm off the pillar axis |
| geometric_l1 | 2 | 2 | 25.5 / 28.9 mm |

Mean over the completed runs (`results/utils/tables/pick_and_place_sim.csv`):

| method | ‖ε‖ max (mm) | ρ max | ‖ε‖ rms (mm) | ρ rms | platform (mm) | EE (mm) | EE heading (°) | q1..q4 (°) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| whole_body_l1 | 142.6 | 0.386 | 27.5 | 0.074 | 27.5 | 29.4 | 0.38 | 0.43 / 2.23 / 1.46 / 1.79 |

set-down (leaving it): the estimator taking up and releasing the 2 N, 10-15 cm on the whole-body rig and
~30 cm on the decoupled one.
