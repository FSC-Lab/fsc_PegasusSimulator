# Simulation results for the paper

Isaac Sim runs of the T650 aerial manipulator, organised for plotting in MATLAB.

## Free-flight trajectory tracking

Three methods are compared:

- `whole_body_l1`: the proposed whole-body L1 impedance controller.
- `geometric_l1`: geometric control with L1 adaptive augmentation, "Geo-L1" (Cai et al., CEP 2025).
- `modular_adaptive`: modular adaptive control, "MAC" (Yadav et al., TMECH 2025).

Each method flies two end-effector trajectories at three mean EE speeds (0.10, 0.13 and 0.20 m/s), three runs each:

- **Circle**: radius 0.5 m.
- **Figure-8**: half-length 0.70 m, half-width 0.35 m, long axis on world x.

Every flight uses the same launch condition and the same plant:

- **Launch**: headless Isaac at real time (RTF 1), raw mocap feedback.
- **Plant**: the sim-to-real mirror plant (config A).
- **Planner**: one lap at time scale 1, EE-run bounds 0.40 m/s² and 1.0 rad/s.
- **Arm sweep**: q2 = 25 ± 15°, four cycles per lap, fold 55°.

Only the DIRECT control law differs between methods. For Geo-L1 the arm also runs in position mode, tracking the planner's joint reference.

# fly (resumes: completed runs on disk are skipped)

### The `.mat` struct `run`

| field | content |
|---|---|
| `meta` | method, shape, nominal mean speed, lap time, trajectory size, arm sweep, plant, controller yaml, driver arguments, flight time, minimum RTF |
| `rmse` | this run's table numbers: platform position x/y/z and norm [mm], platform roll / pitch / heading [deg], EE position x/y/z and norm [mm], EE heading [deg], joints 1-4 [deg], plus CoM position, EE worst sample and peak tilt |
| `tracking` | 100 Hz signals over the trajectory run, `t` in s since the run started: measured, reference and error for the platform position and attitude, CoM, EE position and heading, and joints |
| `raw` | every recorded stream on the driver clock [s]: `odom`, `joints`, `reference` (the planner's whole-body reference), `planner_ee`, `arm_reference`, `controller_debug`, `events` |

Positions are in world ENU [m]. `platform_att_err_deg` is `[roll pitch heading]` in body axes.

### Metric definitions

These are the same definitions as the hardware report (`docs/docs_aerial_manipulator/archive/wb_vs_decoupled_flight_20260928/tools/metrics.py`).

- **Window**: the planner's trajectory run, ramps included, trimmed 0.1 s at each end.
- **Reference**: the planner's whole-body reference, which is identical for all three methods.
  - Platform position: `x_b = x_cd - R0 r_0c(q_d)`.
  - Platform attitude: the compatible attitude `R0` (thrust along `x_cd_ddot + g e3`, heading `b1_d`).
  - EE: position `r_ed`, heading `b1_de`. Joints: `q_d`.
- **Measured**: odometry plus joint states. The EE comes from the planner's own forward kinematics.
- **Errors**:
  - Platform attitude error: the rotation vector of `R_ref^T R`.
  - EE heading error: the azimuth difference.
  - Table cells: the mean over runs of each run's RMSE.

## Payload pick-and-place (2026-10-08/09)

The same scene and task for every controller (`application/robotic_arm/07_px4_t650_aerial_manipulator_pick_and_place.py`):
two 1 m pillars at (1, 1) and (−1, −1) m carrying the printed hat platform (top 1.008 m), the 200 g CAD basket
payload hooked by its wire hanger, the pick-and-place planner's plan (go to start, pick, go to place start,
place, go to land start, land; the hook approach, the clockwise turns, the carry pose). Headless Isaac at
RTF 1, raw mocap, the mirror plant; each controller's `*_sim_pick_and_place.yaml` (the free-flight file + the
pick-and-place gains: whole-body k_R/k_w 1.6/1.2, geometric kp/kv 15/10 and kp_z/kv_z 40/18).

**One timetable for every run (2026-10-09).** The flight driver runs with `--timetable`: every step starts
at the same mission time on every run and every controller, so the six phases are contiguous and line up
across runs (measured: phase starts within 0.01 s). The short hovers between steps are constant slack inside
the phase they end. The planner's `pick_place_descent_trim_first_min: 0` (sim pick-and-place yaml only)
makes the place descent trim sideways first on every run, so it always lasts ~8.2 s; with the hardware's
5 mm default the whole-body rig's 4-6 mm trim flipped between a 3.2 s and an 8.2 s descent. The
2026-10-08 runs, flown without the timetable, are in
`docs/docs_aerial_manipulator/archive/pick_place_top_hat_20261008/runs/unaligned_20261008/` (local only).

- **Window**: the whole mission, from the start of go to start to the end of land, 92.5 s, identical for
  every controller. Phases (s): go to start 0-5.3, pick 5.3-26.9, to place start 26.9-45.9, place

- **State RMSE**: the free-flight definitions (platform position xyz, attitude as the rotation vector of
  R_ref^T R, EE position xyz, EE heading, joints).

## Box pushing (physical interaction, 2026-10-17)

Two controllers on the same plant, scene, mission and timetable: the proposed **whole-body 4-D L1 impedance
controller** and the **coordinated admittance framework** of Liu et al., CEP 146 (2024) 105898 (decoupled UAV
PD + momentum wrench estimator + admittance on the end-effector reference + force-adaptive kinematic MPC, a
position-mode arm; implemented as a comparison rig, `docs/docs_aerial_manipulator/archive/push_pull_coordinated_admittance_20261010/README.md`).

Each grasps the 5 mm fin handle of the CAD box (240 x 160 x 100 mm, **500 g**) on the console table
(x 1.200-1.350, y -0.5..0.5 m, top 0.80 m) and pushes it **0.50 m** toward -y in 24 s (one min-snap move
after a 1.5 s settle): the box's back face starts **0.10 m** from the table's +y end and ends at 0.60 m.
Box / table friction **0.25 static = 0.25 sliding** (1.23 N), from the 2026-10-17 spring-gauge bench test of the
real box on the experiment table. Arm pose in the push **[0, 32, 28, 0] deg** (the 2026-10-16 campaign,
`docs/docs_aerial_manipulator/archive/box_pushing_campaign_20261016/README.md`), the T650 gear, the real arm's q2
range [-20, 45] deg as hard stops (`application/robotic_arm/07_px4_t650_aerial_manipulator_box_pushing.py`).
Exit To Push is the arm GS's sequence on both rigs: the planner releases the claw (`push_pull/release`), the
gripper opens, the exit starts on its OPEN report. Then climb out, one planner transition off the table to the
landing hover (270 deg clockwise), descend. The box guards are on for both rigs: refuse a push that would leave the box
within 3 cm of the table edge; abort on a box > 40 mm behind its plan, a box tilt > 2 deg, q3 < 10 deg, a lost
box feed, a task force > 5 N or a vehicle tilt > 15 deg. Headless Isaac at RTF 1, raw mocap, the mirror plant.

- **Whole-body:** the box-pushing yaml (`..._whole_body_l1_4d_..._sim_box_pushing.yaml`, generated by
  `archive/push_pull_20261003/tools/make_push_pull_yaml.py`: EE impedance K_y 120 / D_y 20.2), one flight per
  attempt by `archive/box_pushing_campaign_20261016/tools/fly.sh`.
- **Coordinated admittance:** its yaml (`..._coordinated_admittance_..._sim_box_pushing.yaml`, generated from
  the whole-body one by that rig's `tools/make_coordinated_yaml.py`: plant, guards and planner block
  verbatim except its gentler planner bounds 0.20 m/s / 0.08 m/s^2 / 0.20 rad/s and its contact flag from
  the jaws' stall to the release), one flight per attempt by its `tools/run_pl_cadm.sh` (15 s hover before Ready,
  the live-box grasp gate).

**One timetable.** `pl_mission.py --timetable` on both rigs: from Ready on every step starts at the same
mission time (land step 40 s after Go To Land Start: the coordinated rig's turn off the table takes 35.9 s,
the whole-body rig's 23.9 s). The methods are aligned at Ready (the coordinated rig's slower first transit
starts before t = 0); the eight phase starts agree across all five runs to 0.8 s.

### Metrics

- **Impedance residual** (the physical-interaction metric): e_imp = F_ext − (M_d ė_v + D_d e_v + K_d e_y)
  per task channel, F_ext the TRUE contact wrench on the claw (the box's PhysX contact report), e_y the
  true claw point minus the streamed reference, every term through one zero-phase 5 Hz low-pass; each rig
  is scored against its own gains (whole-body: the law's M_d / D_d / K_d; coordinated: its contact-phase
  admittance, claw frame, K 150 / 20 / 20 N/m). RMS and max over the 24 s SLIDE, the whole CONTACT, the
  HOLD (grip + settle) and the RELEASE.
- **UAV deviation** and the **state RMSE** as for pick-and-place (window = the whole mission, 99.8 s:
  go to start 0-11.0, ready 11.0-18.0, grasp 18.0-18.9, push 18.9-45.9, release 45.9-46.2, exit 46.2-54.8,
  go to land start 54.8-94.8, land 94.8-99.8 s).
- **The box**: distance pushed (0.50 m commanded), yaw change, tilt, movement during the exit; and the
  release window's joint margins (q2 max, q3 min from the jaws opening to the end of the exit).

## Valve turning (physical interaction, 2026-10-18, final configuration)

The second physical-interaction task, the same two controllers as the box push, on the same plant and one
timetable. Each grasps the 5 mm fin handle of the user's printed **valve mechanism** (Valve_Assembly: a 608
double-sealed bearing on a 150 x 50 x 20 mm plate bolted to the console table's centre, a 225 mm beam with the
fin at **radius 0.160 m**, fin top 0.204 m above the table) and turns the beam **one full revolution (360 deg)
in 36 s** (a smooth start-stop rotation after a 1.5 s settle): the claw world-held on its 0.160 m circle, the
base on a ~0.27 m one behind it, the heading turning with them, the arm held at **[0, 5, 25, 0] deg** (the fin
is low; a more folded arm would put the T650 skids onto the beam). The rotor is flown at the printed part's
**204 g**; the bearing carries a **CONSERVATIVE 0.048 N.m Coulomb friction** (0.30 N at the fin) -- the real
bearing, pulled by a 10 N spring gauge at the fin (2026-10-17), never passed the gauge's first 0.2 N division
(<= 0.032 N.m), and the sim flies 1.5x that bound. Release = the gripper opens with the claw still world-held,
Exit 1 s after the open report (climb, arm home), Go To Land Start, descend. Headless Isaac at RTF 1, raw mocap,
the mirror plant (`application/robotic_arm/07_px4_t650_aerial_manipulator_valve_turning.py`).

**The final valve configuration** (the valve campaign's README section 15.10), the same for both rigs: planner
**tilt guard 10 deg** / task-force guard 5 N, and the **free-handle abort** (hold the planned pose the turn was
cut at, open the jaws with the claw world-held for 2 s, climb straight up); the jaws also open when the node
leaves DIRECT with the claw on the handle. The flight nodes' own watchdog stays at 15 deg.

- **Whole-body:** the valve's OWN yaml (`..._whole_body_l1_4d_..._sim_valve_turning.yaml`, generated by
  `docs/docs_aerial_manipulator/archive/valve_turning_20261015/tools/make_valve_turning_yaml.py`): the box
  push's tune with `wb_l1_omega_c_t` **2.0** and `wb_ee_anchor_blend_s` 1.0 (the 3.0 written earlier was never
  loaded by the node -- valve README 15.4); one flight per attempt by that campaign's `tools/try_valve.sh`.
- **Coordinated admittance:** its yaml generated from the whole-body one (`make_coordinated_yaml.py
  --profile valve_turning`, REGENERATED 2026-10-18 so it carries the guards and abort above); one flight per
  attempt by `tools/try_valve_cadm.sh`.

**One timetable** (`pl_mission.py --timetable`, release 39 s after the Turn starts, land 40 s after Go To Land
Start: the coordinated rig's transition takes 35.9 s, the whole-body rig's 23.9 s).
